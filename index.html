02 / Choose your path

Two tracks, one mission.

Coding track

AI Hospital Challenge

Build an AI that monitors simulated ward vitals — heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen saturation — and flags patient risk early. Your model watches in real time and alerts staff when something looks wrong. Perfect for first-timers and clinicians new to ML; workshops and mentors support every skill level.

Join coding track →
Pitching track

EMR Data Challenge

Work hands-on with de-identified hospital EMR data and industry mentors to tackle high-impact problems from the front lines. Through guided interviews you'll surface a critical bottleneck, break it apart, build a compelling demo, and pitch it to a panel of expert judges.

Join pitching track →
Not a coder? You're still in the right room. Pair up with builders and bring the clinical insight, product thinking or pitching — clinicians and non-technical teammates are the secret weapon on most winning teams.

08 / Good questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need prior experience in AI or healthcare?
Not at all. The Coding Track is designed for all skill levels, from newcomers to experienced developers. Mentors and workshops will get you up to speed.
Can I form a team beforehand?
Absolutely — we encourage it. The Pre-Hack Networking Night on 17 July is the perfect place to find your co-hackers. Solo attendees can also join teams on Day 1.
Is the data real and safe to use?
Yes. All real data is fully de-identified and complies with strict privacy regulations. For the Coding Track, simulated data ensures a safe learning environment.
What are the team size requirements?
Teams should be a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 6 members. We recommend 5–6 for the full experience.
Will food and coffee be provided?
Yes! Meals and refreshments are provided throughout the hackathon weekend, so you can focus on building.
Can I do both tracks?
Yes — and we encourage it. Doing both gives you the full HealthHack experience: build a technical solution and pitch your ideas to judges.
Do I need to attend both days?
Both Day 1 (18 July) and Day 2 (19 July) are required — Day 2 is when final pitches and the prize ceremony happen.
How much are tickets?
Hackathon tickets are $64.79 early-bird or $96.40 late-bird, and one ticket covers both tracks across the weekend. Students get 70% off the early-bird price with code IAMSTUDENT (about $19.44). The optional Pre-Hack Networking night on 17 July is a separate $5 ticket. Tickets are limited and sell out, so earlier is cheaper — grab yours on our Luma page.
What should I bring?
Your laptop and charger, a photo ID, and any tools or accounts you like to build with. The data, mentors, food and coffee are all covered.
Is this only for UNSW students?
Not at all. HealthHack is open to everyone — students from any university, working professionals, clinicians and community members are all welcome.
Is there an online participation option?
This is an in-person event — at least one team member must be on-site. If you have exceptional circumstances, reach out to us.
09 / The crew

Who are we?

UNSW No Code Society

A student-led organisation empowering the next generation of builders, makers and innovators. We run workshops, hackathons and networking events that help anyone — regardless of technical background — learn to build products and solve problems with modern tools. HealthHack Sydney is our flagship event.

MLAI

A not-for-profit fostering a vibrant community passionate about AI. With over 15,000 members — data scientists, AI developers, healthcare professionals and students — MLAI makes AI accessible, fun and impactful. From monthly lightning talks to large-scale hackathons, MLAI's events are consistently oversubscribed.
